# Merchant outreach playbook (Phase 0)

Direct outreach is the highest-signal, no-gatekeeper channel: no karma, no
follower count, no algorithm. 10 real conversations beat 1,000 impressions.
Goal: **book 5 short calls** or get **5 "I'd use/pay for this"**.

## Ideal target (who to contact)

- Runs a **Shopify** store doing real volume (not a brand-new dropship test).
- Already cares about email — bonus if they mention **Klaviyo / Mailchimp / Omnisend**.
- Small-to-mid team (a founder or a marketer who feels the bill personally).
- Niche where repeat purchase / cart recovery matters: apparel, beauty,
  supplements, coffee, accessories, home goods.

Skip: huge enterprises (won't self-host), dead stores, pure-Amazon sellers.

## Where to find them (free)

1. **BuiltWith / Wappalyzer free trials** — filter "Shopify + Klaviyo" tech stacks.
2. **r/ecommerce, r/shopify** — people literally posting about email costs; reply
   helpfully, then DM. (Reading/commenting works even on a new account.)
3. **Twitter/X search** — queries like `Klaviyo expensive`, `abandoned cart`,
   `Shopify email` — find frustrated store owners, reply, then DM.
4. **Shopify Community forums** — email/marketing threads.
5. **Indie e-commerce Discords / Slack groups** (e.g. eCommerceFuel-style).
6. **Instagram/TikTok shops** running Shopify — DM the business account.

Contact via the store's **public business channel** (support email, business DM,
contact form) — business info, not personal data. Don't scrape private emails.

## The message

Lead with **curiosity, not a pitch**. You're researching, not selling.

### First touch (DM / email)
> Subject: quick question about your email setup
>
> Hey [name] — I run into a lot of Shopify stores frustrated that their email
> bill (Klaviyo etc.) climbs as their list grows. Quick, honest question — not a
> pitch: what do you use for abandoned-cart/email right now, and roughly what
> does it cost you per month?
>
> I'm building an open-source, self-hostable alternative and I'd rather learn
> from real store owners than build the wrong thing. Would a 10-min call be too
> much to ask? Either way, happy to share it when it's ready.

### If they reply with interest
> Awesome, thank you. Here's the early landing + repo so you can see the
> direction: https://flowmail-six.vercel.app/waitlist
> Two quick things I'd love your take on:
> 1) Would self-hosting appeal, or is managed hosting a must-have for you?
> 2) What's the one email automation you'd never want to lose?

### Follow-up (if no reply after 3–4 days)
> Just floating this back up — no worries if now's not a good time. Even a
> one-line "we use X, pay $Y" would help me a ton. 🙏

## Rules of the road

- **Personalize the first line** every time (their store, product, a recent post).
  Copy-paste blasts get ignored and can get you flagged.
- One message, then **one** follow-up. Never more.
- Respect "no" instantly. You're building reputation, not just a list.
- Send 5–10 per day, not 100 — quality over volume.
