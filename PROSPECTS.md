# Prospecting starter (Phase 0)

Use this with [OUTREACH.md](OUTREACH.md). Goal: find **right-sized** Shopify
stores (small-to-mid, founder/marketer feels the email bill) — NOT mega-brands.

## Reality check on targets

Big, famous brands (Glossier, Kylie Cosmetics, Death Wish, Stumptown, Trade)
show up on every "best Shopify stores" list — but they have teams, contracts,
and **won't self-host or answer a cold DM**. They're useful as *proof the niche
uses Klaviyo*, not as outreach targets.

**Your sweet spot:** independent brands roughly ~5–50 people, a few hundred to a
few thousand orders/month, where the founder still reads the inbox.

## Best sources to build a real list (free)

- **StoreInspect — companies using Klaviyo:** https://storeinspect.com/companies-using/klaviyo
- **StoreInspect — beauty Shopify stores:** https://storeinspect.com/top-shopify-stores/beauty
- **BuiltWith / Wappalyzer:** filter "Shopify + Klaviyo", then sort for smaller sites.
- **r/ecommerce & r/shopify:** people literally posting "Klaviyo is too expensive" — DM them.
- **X search:** `Klaviyo expensive`, `abandoned cart`, `Shopify email` → reply, then DM.
- **Niche Discords / FB groups** for DTC founders.

Workflow: open a store → confirm it's Shopify + Klaviyo (Wappalyzer) → judge size
(product count, review volume, socials) → find the **public** contact (support
email / business DM / contact form) → personalize the OUTREACH.md message.

## Example brands by niche (for inspiration + to study their email)

> Verify size/stack yourself before reaching out. Big names = study, not cold-DM.

**Beauty / skincare (heavy Klaviyo usage):**
- Forever Beaumore — K/Asian-beauty, mid-size, syncs data into Klaviyo
- Sukoshi Mart — K-beauty, Gen-Z audience
- (mega, study-only: Glossier, Kylie Cosmetics)

**Coffee / F&B:**
- BLK & Bold — independent, mission-driven
- (mega, study-only: Death Wish, Stumptown, Trade)

**How to expand this list:** pick ONE niche, pull 20–30 stores from StoreInspect's
Klaviyo list, drop the giants, and keep ~15 right-sized ones in the OUTREACH.md
tracking table.

## Don't

- Don't scrape or store personal emails — use public business contacts only.
- Don't blast identical DMs — personalize the first line every time.
- Don't chase the giants; 15 reachable founders beat 3 ignored brands.
